A little about me

My analytical approach

I approach data analysis by starting with the business problem, not the dashboard. I focus on understanding what stakeholders need to know, checking whether the data can answer their questions, validating the underlying calculations, and communicating findings clearly.


My goal is to produce analysis that is not only visually clear but also accurate, traceable, and useful for decision-making.

How I work

Analysis before presentation

Understand the question. Clarify the business problem and what a useful answer looks like.

Validate the data. Inspect the data quality, definitions, relationships, and level of detail.

Build reliable analysis. Transform the data, model it appropriately, and verify calculations.

Communicate the findings. Explain what the results mean and what deserves management's attention.

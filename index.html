How I Work

My Approach to Data Analysis

A reliable report starts long before the first chart. My process prioritizes understanding the question and ensuring the data can support the answer.

01. Understand the Business Problem

Clarify what the stakeholder wants to know, why the answer matters, and what decision the analysis should support.

02. Inspect and Validate the Data

Examine table structures, data types, missing values, duplicates, and the meaning of each record.

03. Build a Reliable Data Model

Establish the correct grain, define fact and dimension tables, and create relationships that support accurate results.

04. Analyze and Communicate Findings

Use SQL, measures, and appropriate visuals to answer business questions, explain the findings, and identify practical next steps.
